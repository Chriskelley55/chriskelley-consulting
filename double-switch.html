How it opens

The prayers came up over the outfield fence a little after two in the morning, Houston time. They always came up slow. Wobbling, catching the light, turning end over end like foul balls hit straight back into deep bleachers. Most nights there were millions of them. Tonight there were millions of them too, but two of them came up out of the same city at the exact same speed, and that was the part worth noticing.
