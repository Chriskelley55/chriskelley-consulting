A dying breed in a newly formed country.

Post-civil war, the United States has fractured into four nations. Adrian Kuldell — a NAVO, a non-automated vehicle operator — runs his family's delivery fleet out of Houston, Texas with the help of his AI, Chip. Business is declining. The fleet is aging. And somewhere beneath it all, a bigger question: what actually happened to his parents?

How it opens

Jay and Rusty traveled up a traversing highway in a mountainous valley. Tall pines and aspens lined the road like columns — leaving a window of stars above them in the night's sky.

Inside the dimly lit cabin of their autonomously driven truck, subtle neon mood lights slowly cycled through violet, indigo, blue and green. An animated route traced the road's bends on the heads-up display while indicating the vehicle's speed in miles per hour on the windshield.
